About Kbong

A focused software company, built around one idea: build exactly what's ordered

Our Approach

Software should fit the business, not the other way around

Kbong Pte. Ltd. was founded on a simple premise: too many businesses are forced to reshape their operations around rigid, generic software because building something custom was assumed to be too slow, too expensive, or too risky.

We work differently. Every project is order-on-demand — scoped from your actual requirements, quoted transparently, and built by a dedicated team accountable for the outcome. No forced platform, no unnecessary features, no compromise on how your business already works.

What We Stand For

The principles behind every engagement

Precision Scoping

We won't start building until the requirements are clear and agreed — for both sides.

Transparent Delivery

Fixed pricing, milestone reviews, and no surprise scope changes.

Confidentiality

Client data, business logic, and source code are treated as strictly confidential.

You Own What We Build

Source code, documentation, and infrastructure access are handed over at delivery — no lock-in.
