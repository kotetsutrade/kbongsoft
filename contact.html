Frequently Asked

Before you reach out

Do you take on small projects, or only large ones?

We take on projects of varying size, as long as the scope is well defined. Reach out with your idea and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

How is pricing determined?

After the requirements discussion, we provide a fixed quotation tied to defined milestones — no open-ended hourly billing.

Who owns the source code after delivery?

You do. Source code, documentation, and deployment access are handed over in full at project completion.

Can you support the system after launch?

Yes — we offer ongoing support and enhancement retainers for clients who want a continued partnership after go-live.
