Our Process

A structured path from request to running software

Order-on-demand development only works with a disciplined process. Here is exactly how an engagement with Kbong Pte. Ltd. runs, start to finish.

1
Step 1

Requirements & Discovery

We start with structured conversations with your team to understand the business problem, current workflow, and what "done" looks like. This typically covers user roles, data to be tracked, key workflows, integrations needed, and any constraints (compliance, existing systems, timeline). The output is a written requirements document both sides agree on before anything else proceeds.

2
Step 2

Proposal, Architecture & Quotation

Based on the confirmed requirements, we produce a technical proposal: system architecture, technology choices, a delivery timeline broken into milestones, and a fixed quotation. You know the scope and cost before we write a single line of code.

3
Step 3

Design & Prototyping

For anything with a significant user interface, we produce wireframes or clickable prototypes for sign-off before full development begins — so structural feedback happens early, when it's cheap to act on.

4
Step 4

Development in Milestone Sprints

Development proceeds in milestone-based cycles, each ending with a working build you can review. Regular checkpoints keep the build aligned with what was ordered, and let you raise adjustments before the next cycle starts.

5
Step 5

Quality Assurance & User Acceptance Testing

Before launch, the system goes through structured QA against the original requirements, followed by a user acceptance testing period where your team validates the software against real scenarios.

6
Step 6

Deployment & Handover

We deploy the system to production, migrate any existing data, and hand over full documentation and source code — you own what we build.

7
Step 7

Ongoing Support & Iteration

Once live, we offer support plans for monitoring, bug fixes, and future enhancement requests — so the system keeps pace as your business changes.

Engagement Models

Ways to work with us

Fixed-Scope Project

A clearly defined system, delivered against a fixed price and timeline. Best when requirements are well understood upfront.

Phased Delivery

Larger systems broken into independently deliverable phases, so you see value early and can adjust direction between phases.

Ongoing Retainer

Continued development and support on a retainer basis for clients with evolving or long-term software needs.
