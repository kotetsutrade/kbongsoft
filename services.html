Services

Software engineering delivered exactly to your specification

Every engagement is order-on-demand — we don't push pre-built products. Below is the range of work we typically take on; if what you need isn't listed, tell us anyway.

Custom Application Development

Web, desktop, and mobile applications designed from a blank page around your exact requirements.

We build software the way you'd commission a piece of custom equipment: you specify what it must do, and we engineer it accordingly. This spans customer-facing web applications, internal desktop tools, and mobile apps for field teams — architected for the exact data model and user roles your business needs, rather than adapted from a generic starting point.

Web applications & internal portals
Desktop & Windows/Mac utilities
Mobile apps (iOS / Android)
Custom reporting & dashboards

Business & Customer Management Systems

Internal platforms that track your customers, deals, accounts, and service history in one place.

A common request we receive: a central system for managing customer records, sales pipelines, follow-ups, and support history — built around the specific way your team already sells and services accounts, rather than forcing you into a rigid, one-size-fits-all product. We've delivered systems of this kind for organisations that needed more structure than spreadsheets could offer but didn't want to reshape their process around someone else's software.

Contact & account record-keeping
Sales pipeline & deal tracking
Task, follow-up & reminder workflows
Role-based access for teams
Custom reporting on customer activity
Support & service ticket tracking

Workflow & Process Automation

Turning manual, error-prone processes into structured, automated digital workflows.

If a process today runs through email threads, shared spreadsheets, or manual approvals, we can typically model it as a proper digital workflow — with clear stages, ownership, and an audit trail. This reduces errors, speeds up turnaround, and gives management visibility they didn't have before.

Approval & sign-off workflows
Document generation & processing
Scheduled jobs & notifications
Data validation & reconciliation tools

Systems Integration & APIs

Making the tools you already use talk to each other.

Most businesses run on a patchwork of accounting, payment, logistics, and messaging tools. We build the integration layer — APIs, webhooks, and data pipelines — that lets these systems share information automatically, so your team stops re-entering the same data in three places.

Payment gateway integration
Accounting & ERP connectors
Third-party & partner APIs
Custom internal API design

Cloud Infrastructure & DevOps

Reliable, secure infrastructure for the software we build for you.

Software is only as good as the infrastructure it runs on. We provision cloud environments, set up deployment pipelines, and put monitoring in place so your system stays available, performs well under load, and can be updated safely over time.

Cloud provisioning (AWS / GCP / Azure)
CI/CD pipeline setup
Monitoring & alerting
Backup & disaster recovery planning

Legacy System Modernization

Rebuilding ageing systems without disrupting the business that depends on them.

Old systems that are hard to maintain, slow to extend, or built on unsupported technology don't have to be replaced overnight. We plan and execute phased modernization — re-architecting the system piece by piece so operations continue uninterrupted while the underlying technology is brought up to date.

Technical audit & migration planning
Database & platform migration
Incremental re-architecture
Post-migration support
